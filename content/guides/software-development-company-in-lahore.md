## What does a software development company in Lahore do?

A full-service software house takes a business idea from concept to a live, maintained product. The core responsibilities are:

- **Discovery and scoping** — turning business goals into a clear technical specification, roadmap, and estimate.
- **UI/UX design** — wireframes, interactive prototypes, and a design system before a single line of production code.
- **Engineering** — building the frontend, backend, APIs, and databases that power the product.
- **Quality assurance** — manual and automated testing so the software works on real devices and real edge cases.
- **Deployment and DevOps** — shipping to cloud infrastructure (AWS, Vercel) with CI/CD pipelines.
- **Maintenance and support** — bug fixes, security patches, performance tuning, and new features after launch.

A genuine software development company owns all of these stages, rather than reselling templates or stopping at design.

## How to choose a software development company in Lahore

Lahore has hundreds of software houses, from solo freelancers to large agencies. Use this buyer's checklist to separate genuine engineering partners from resellers:

- **Look past the homepage stats.** Many sites show impressive but blank or unverifiable counters. Ask for specifics you can verify.
- **Check who actually does the work.** Confirm the team is in-house, not silently subcontracted, and ask who your day-to-day contact will be.
- **Demand a clear process.** A real partner can walk you through discovery, design, sprints, QA, and support. Vagueness here is a red flag.
- **Insist on transparent pricing.** You should get an itemised estimate and a defined scope — not just "it depends."
- **Confirm communication cadence and timezone fit.** Ask how often you'll get demos and updates, and in which timezone they work.
- **Verify code ownership and IP.** Make sure the contract assigns full IP and source-code ownership to you.
- **Plan for after launch.** Ask what maintenance, security patching, and support look like once you go live.
- **Talk to a human first.** A short discovery call tells you more about fit than any award badge.

We built this page to pass our own checklist — clear process, honest pricing ranges, and a real team you can talk to.
