## Which engagement model is right for you?

Different projects need different commercial shapes. Below are the four models we offer with **typical market rate ranges** so you can budget before the first call. Offshore teams (including ours, based in Pakistan) generally sit at the lower end; US and Western Europe-based talent sit at the top.

| **Team (squad)** | Larger builds needing dev + QA + lead | Scoped per role, blended monthly rate |

| **Fixed-scope project** | Defined deliverable: new theme, migration, app | Quoted against milestones and scope |

### What drives the cost?

- **Location of the talent.** This is the single biggest lever. Offshore Shopify developers in South Asia typically run **$20-$45/hr**; Eastern Europe and Latin America **$30-$50/hr**; US-based senior developers **$90-$200/hr**, with enterprise agencies higher still.
- **Seniority.** Junior theme tweaks cost far less than a senior building Shopify Functions or a headless storefront.
- **Surface complexity.** Standard Online Store 2.0 work is cheaper than Shopify Plus, checkout extensibility, or B2B engineering.
- **Engagement length.** Dedicated monthly retainers usually carry a lower effective hourly rate than one-off hourly work.

Rates above are **typical market ranges** drawn from public 2026 pricing guides, not a fixed Web On Dev price list. You receive a specific quote after a short scoping call.

## Should you hire an agency, a freelancer, or hire in-house?

Each route trades cost, risk, and control differently. Here is an honest comparison.

| Factor | Web On Dev (agency) | Freelancer | In-house hire |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Time to start** | Days | Days to weeks | 1-3 months |
| **Vetting** | Done for you | You vet | You vet & interview |
| **Cost (offshore)** | Mid; team rate | Lowest hourly | Highest (salary + overhead) |
| **Coverage if dev is out** | Backup & lead in place | Single point of failure | Cover by colleagues |
| **Scale up/down** | Flexible, fast | Limited | Slow (hire/lay off) |
| **Specialist depth (Plus, headless)** | On the team | Varies widely | Hard to hire one person for all |
| **Accountability** | Delivery lead owns it | Self-managed | Manager owns it |
| **Best when** | You need reliable, scalable Shopify capacity | Tight budget, small one-off task | Shopify is core, long-term, full-time |

If Shopify is a permanent core function and you can wait months to recruit, in-house can make sense. If you need senior capacity now, with cover and the ability to scale, a vetted agency team is usually the lower-risk path.
