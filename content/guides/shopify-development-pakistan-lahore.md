## What Shopify services does Web On Dev offer?

We cover the full Shopify lifecycle — from a first store launch to enterprise Shopify Plus and headless rebuilds.

### Shopify store setup and launch
Complete store builds: theme configuration, product and collection structure, payment and shipping setup, taxes, domains, and launch QA. Ideal for new brands going live for the first time.

### Custom Shopify themes and Liquid development
Bespoke theme design and front-end builds in **Liquid**, Shopify's templating language — plus customization of premium themes (Dawn and OS 2.0 themes) so your store looks and behaves exactly the way your brand needs, not like a template.

### Shopify app integration and custom apps
Integrating the apps your operations depend on — reviews, subscriptions, loyalty, ERP/inventory, shipping, email/SMS, analytics — and building **custom Shopify apps** when an off-the-shelf app does not exist or does not fit.

### Migration to Shopify
Moving from WooCommerce, Magento, BigCommerce, Wix or a custom platform onto Shopify — products, variants, customers, order history, SEO URLs and 301 redirects — without losing rankings or data.

### Shopify Plus development
Enterprise builds on **Shopify Plus**: Scripts, Functions, B2B/wholesale, multi-store and multi-currency, checkout extensibility, and the automation high-volume merchants need.

### Speed optimization and CRO
Core Web Vitals tuning, theme/script cleanup, image and lazy-load optimization, and conversion-rate work (PDP, cart and checkout UX) so traffic actually turns into revenue.

### Headless Shopify with Next.js
This is our differentiator. We pair Shopify's commerce engine (via the **Storefront API**) with a **Next.js** front end for a fast, fully custom, SEO-strong storefront. Headless suits brands that have outgrown Liquid theming and want app-like performance and total design control. *(See our [e-commerce solutions](/ecommerce-solutions/) for the full picture.)*

### 3. How much does it cost to build a Shopify store in Pakistan?
Typical ranges run from about **$700–$2,500** for a starter store, **$2,500–$8,000+** for a custom Liquid theme, and **$10,000–$40,000+** for a headless Next.js build. These are indicative; we confirm a fixed or estimated price in writing after a discovery call.

### 4. Can you migrate my existing store to Shopify?
Yes. We migrate from WooCommerce, Magento, BigCommerce, Wix and custom platforms — moving products, variants, customers, order history and SEO URLs, with 301 redirects to protect your rankings.

### 5. What is headless Shopify, and do I need it?
Headless means Shopify runs the commerce engine (cart, checkout, catalog) while a separate **Next.js** front end renders your storefront via the Storefront API. It gives app-like speed and total design freedom. You likely need it if you have outgrown standard theming or want a highly custom, very fast site; many merchants are well served by a well-built Liquid theme, and we will tell you honestly which fits.

### 6. Do you work with the time zones of US, UK, UAE and Australia?
Yes. Pakistan Standard Time (UTC+5) overlaps strongly with UK/EU and UAE afternoons and with US East Coast mornings and Australian business hours — enabling real-time calls and same-day turnarounds, not 24-hour delays.

### 7. Do you provide ongoing support after launch?
Yes. We offer monthly care plans and retainers for maintenance, app updates, speed/CRO optimization and new features — starting from around **$400/month**, scoped to your needs.
